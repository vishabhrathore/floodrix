## 2. What data we collect

### 2a. Analytics data (only with your consent)

If you accept analytics cookies, we collect the following via Google Analytics 4 (GA4) through Google Tag Manager:

- Pages visited and time spent on each page
- How you arrived at the site (search engine, direct, referral)
- Browser type, operating system, and screen resolution
- Approximate geographic location (country and city — derived from IP address; the full IP address is not stored by us)
- Events such as button clicks, file downloads, and form interactions

This data is aggregated and anonymised. It is used solely to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve content and navigation. **We do not build individual user profiles.**

### 2b. Contact and enquiry data

When you submit a project enquiry or contact form, we collect:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your organisation name (if provided)
- The content of your message

This data is used only to respond to your enquiry and is not used for marketing without your separate consent.

### 2c. Cookie preference

We store your cookie consent choice (accepted or rejected) in your browser's local storage. This is a functional necessity to remember your preference across visits and does not itself constitute tracking.

### 2d. Data we do not collect

We do not collect:

- Payment card or financial information
- Sensitive personal data (health, religion, ethnicity, etc.)
- Data from children under 18
- Precise geolocation

## 4. Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager

We use Google Tag Manager (GTM) to manage analytics tags. GTM itself does not collect personal data; it only loads scripts when permitted.

Google Analytics 4 is loaded **only after you explicitly accept analytics cookies**. If you reject or have not yet made a choice, no GA4 data collection occurs.

Google processes analytics data on servers that may be located outside India, including in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and implements Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers. For details, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We have enabled **IP anonymisation** in our GA4 configuration. We do not enable Google Signals or advertising features.

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and applicable law, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
